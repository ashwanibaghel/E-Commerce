# Code of Conduct

This project is maintained for professional ecommerce demo work. Contributors and collaborators are expected to keep communication respectful, practical and focused on the quality of the client experience.

## Expected Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep client and customer data private.
- Avoid committing secrets, credentials or local database exports.
- Raise risks clearly when a change affects checkout, orders, payments or customer data.
- Keep demo content appropriate for a business presentation.

## Reporting

For concerns related to this repository, contact the project maintainer directly.
